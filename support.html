Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea for ChordMentor? Email chordmentor@nickcoleman.io and say which device you're using.

Common questions

I downloaded ChordMentor early. Do I have Pro? Yes, if your first download was before the Black Friday deal for early adopters ended. Update to the latest version and open Settings › ChordMentor Pro: it says Pro is yours to keep. It needs an internet connection the first time.

How do I get Pro on my other devices? Sign in with the same Apple Account. A bought Pro comes back with Settings › ChordMentor Pro › Restore Purchases; the early-adopter deal turns on by itself.

Sprint doesn't hear my strums. Make sure ChordMentor has microphone access in your device's Settings, keep the device within arm's reach of the guitar, and raise Sensitivity in Sprint's Options.

Where are the tips? Settings › Show tips turns them on or off. A tip's × closes just that one; Settings brings back any you closed.

What does Pro cost? ChordMentor is free to use. Pro is a one-time purchase, and the price is shown in the app.
