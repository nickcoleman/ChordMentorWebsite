Support

How can I help?

Questions, a bug, or an idea for ChordMentor? Email chordmentor@nickcoleman.io and say which device you’re using.

For step-by-step help with the main screens, see the guides.

Common questions

Do I have ChordMentor Pro?
Yes. Everyone who downloads ChordMentor now gets Pro as an early adopter, and it’s theirs to keep. Settings › ChordMentor Pro says so. The app needs an internet connection the first time it checks. If you downloaded an earlier version, update to the latest one.
How do I get Pro on my other devices?
Sign in with the same Apple Account and Pro turns on by itself. If it doesn’t, open Settings › ChordMentor Pro › Restore Purchases.
Will Pro cost money later?
Later on, Pro will be sold as a one-time purchase, with the price shown in the app. If you already have Pro as an early adopter, you keep it.
What do I need?
An iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later, or a Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later, plus a guitar. The microphone is only used in Sprint’s Listen mode. Counting in your head works without it.
How do I change my starting level?
Open Settings › Help › Welcome and Starting Level. On a Mac, it’s in the Help menu too.
Does ChordMentor check my timing?
No. It gives you a guide guitar, a metronome and beat lights to play along with, and you decide how clean each change felt. Sprint counts your chord changes in a minute. It doesn’t grade them.
Sprint doesn’t hear my strums.
Make sure ChordMentor has microphone access in your device’s Settings, keep the device within arm’s reach of the guitar, and raise Sensitivity in Sprint’s Options.
Where are the tips?
Settings › Show tips turns them on or off. A tip’s × closes just that one, and Settings brings back any you closed.
How do I rate ChordMentor?
Settings › About › Rate ChordMentor… opens the App Store’s review page. Thank you. It really helps.
