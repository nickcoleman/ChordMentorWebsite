Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea for ChordMentor? Email chordmentor@nickcoleman.io and say which device you're using.

Common questions

How do I get Pro on my other devices? Open ChordMentor › Settings › Restore Purchases on each device, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Sprint doesn't hear my strums. Make sure ChordMentor has microphone access in your device's Settings, keep the device within arm's reach of the guitar, and raise Sensitivity in Sprint's Options.

Where are the tips? Settings › Show tips turns them on or off. A tip's × closes just that one; Settings brings back any you closed.

Is there a subscription? No. ChordMentor is free to use, and Pro is a single purchase.
