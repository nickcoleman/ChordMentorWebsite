Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026.

ChordMentor collects no personal information. There is no account to create, no tracking, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Your drills, strum and picking patterns, saved and drawn chord shapes, starting level, practice history and settings are stored on your device. ChordMentor doesn’t send them anywhere.

The microphone

In a Sprint’s Listen mode, ChordMentor uses the microphone to hear your guitar and count your chord changes. The sound is analyzed on your device as you play and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. You can turn off microphone access at any time in your device’s Settings, and the rest of the app works without it.

Purchases

Right now nothing is for sale in ChordMentor: everyone who downloads it gets ChordMentor Pro as an early adopter. To check this, ChordMentor asks the App Store, on your device, when you first downloaded the app. The answer stays on your device and is never sent to us.

When ChordMentor Pro is sold, it will be bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your payment details. Pro reaches your other devices through your Apple Account, not through anything we store.

Rating the app

Settings › About › Rate ChordMentor… opens the App Store. ChordMentor doesn’t see what you write there.

Children

ChordMentor collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: email chordmentor@nickcoleman.io, or see the Support page.
