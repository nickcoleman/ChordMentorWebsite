Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026.

ChordMentor collects no personal information. There is no account to create, no tracking, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Your drills, strum patterns, saved and drawn chord shapes, practice history and settings are stored on your device. ChordMentor doesn't send them anywhere.

The microphone

In a Sprint's Listen mode, ChordMentor uses the microphone to hear your guitar and count your chord changes. The sound is analyzed on your device as you play and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. You can turn off microphone access at any time in your device's Settings; the rest of the app works without it.

Purchases

ChordMentor Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. Pro reaches your other devices through your Apple Account, not through anything we store.

Children

ChordMentor collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
